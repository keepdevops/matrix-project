**Epic:** MS-130 (research — Metal embed spike)
**Points:** 1
**Branch:** `main`
**Decision date:** 2026-06-02

> ⛔ **SUPERSEDED by [MS-160](MS-160-concurrency-scope.md) (2026-06-03).** This
> GO was measured on **single-stream** throughput only. The MS-160 concurrency
> gate found naive in-process generation **crashes at 2 concurrent agents**
> (MLX default Metal stream is not thread-safe). The +107% single-stream win
> does not survive the swarm's multi-agent workload. **MS-161 must not open**
> until a prerequisite spike proves per-thread MLX stream isolation works.

## Decision: **GO** (single-stream only — see banner; concurrency = NO-GO)

All three MS-154 criteria are met **for single-stream inference**. MS-161
(~50 pts) is **not** approved for the multi-agent swarm until the MS-160
concurrency blocker is resolved.

| Criterion | Threshold | Source | Result |
|-----------|-----------|--------|--------|
| tok/s improvement | ≥30% over HTTP baseline (56 tok/s) | MS-153 (reproducible) | **+107%** (116 tok/s steady-state, two runs) ✅ |
| Memory regression | No significant RSS increase | MS-153 | **Neutral** (~2.33 GB; replaces sidecar, not additive) ✅ |
| Output parity | Same weights / same generate path | MS-153 | **Deterministic + structural parity** ✅ |

## Evidence trail

| Ticket | Finding |
|--------|---------|
| MS-151 | CMake scaffold compiles cleanly; Metal available on M3 Max |
| MS-152 | First spike ~85 tok/s — **superseded** (mid-warmup snapshot, did not reproduce) |
| MS-153 | **Source of record.** Load-once + N warm iterations via `mlx_bench_probe`: steady-state **116 tok/s (+107%)** across two runs; cold-within-process ~111 (+98%); peak RSS ~2.33 GB (neutral — replaces the `mlx_lm.server` sidecar); deterministic output. The cold *filesystem*-cache first launch was a −9.8% one-off, irrelevant to a persistent coordinator. |

> **Why +107%, not the earlier +51.8%/+96%:** those were measured across
> separate process launches whose throughput was dominated by OS page-cache
> warming of the model-file load, not inference. MS-153 loads once and measures
> steady-state in-process — the condition a real coordinator runs in.
